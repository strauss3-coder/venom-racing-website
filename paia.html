01 Purpose of this page

The Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 ("PAIA") gives everyone the right to request access to records held by a private body where the record is required to exercise or protect a right. This page sets out how to make such a request to Venom Racing.

03 The Information Regulator's guide

The Information Regulator publishes a guide on how to use PAIA and POPIA, in all official languages. It is available from the Regulator's website at inforegulator.org.za and from its offices at JD House, 27 Stiemens Street, Braamfontein, Johannesburg, 2001.

04 Records we hold

Venom Racing holds records in the following categories:

  • Business records - registration, accreditation (including RMI) and dealership documents.
  • Financial records - accounting records, invoices, tax returns and banking records.
  • Customer records - enquiries, quotes, job cards, vehicle and work records, invoices and correspondence.
  • Supplier records - agreements, orders and correspondence with suppliers and service providers.
  • Employee records - employment contracts, payroll and statutory records.
  • Operational records - workshop procedures, equipment and calibration records, and health and safety records.

The content of this website is available to everyone without a request.

05 Records kept under other laws

We keep records as required by laws that include the Basic Conditions of Employment Act, the Compensation for Occupational Injuries and Diseases Act, the Consumer Protection Act, the Income Tax Act, the Labour Relations Act, the Occupational Health and Safety Act, the Protection of Personal Information Act, the Tax Administration Act, the Unemployment Insurance Act and the Value-Added Tax Act.

06 How to request access

  1. Complete the prescribed request form for a private body (Form 2 under the PAIA Regulations, available from the Information Regulator's website).
  2. Send it to our Information Officer at venom@venomracing.co.za or deliver it to our workshop.
  3. Identify the record, the form of access you want, and the right you are trying to exercise or protect.
  4. Pay any prescribed request and access fees when asked. We will tell you what applies before any cost is incurred.

We will decide on your request within 30 days, which may be extended once by up to 30 days where the Act allows, and tell you the reason.

07 Grounds for refusal

PAIA requires or allows us to refuse access in certain cases, including to protect another person's privacy, commercial information of a third party or of Venom Racing, confidential information, the safety of individuals and property, and records privileged from production in legal proceedings. If we refuse, we will give you our reasons.

08 If you are unhappy with a decision

You may complain to the Information Regulator within 180 days of our decision, or apply to court for relief.
