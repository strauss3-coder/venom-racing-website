01 General information

The content on venomracing.co.za is provided for general information only. Although we take care to keep it accurate and current, vehicles and regulations vary, and the information may not apply to your vehicle. Please speak to us before acting on it.

02 Performance results

Before-and-after figures, dyno results and gains described on the website were measured on particular vehicles under particular conditions. Dyno readings also vary between dynamometers and with temperature, altitude and fuel. They show what is possible, not what any specific vehicle will achieve.

03 Vehicle modifications

Performance modifications, ECU calibration and exhaust changes can affect:

  • your manufacturer's warranty;
  • your insurance cover, which usually requires you to declare modifications;
  • roadworthiness, emissions and noise compliance under the National Road Traffic Act and local by-laws;
  • the wear and service life of engine and drivetrain components.

Some products and calibrations are intended for off-road or motorsport use only. We will explain the implications of any work before we start, and it remains your responsibility to use your vehicle lawfully.
