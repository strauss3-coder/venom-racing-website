At a glance

  • Our Information Officer is responsible for POPIA compliance.
  • We collect only what we need, for a clear purpose, and keep it secure.
  • You may access, correct, delete or object to the use of your information.
  • You may complain to the Information Regulator at any time.

01 Our commitment

Venom Racing respects your right to privacy. We process personal information lawfully, reasonably and only for the purposes we explain in our Privacy Policy.

02 Information Officer

As a private body, Venom Racing's Information Officer is the head of the business, who is responsible for encouraging and overseeing compliance with POPIA and for dealing with requests from data subjects and the Information Regulator.

03 The eight conditions in practice

ConditionWhat we do
AccountabilityOur Information Officer oversees compliance, and these policies are reviewed when our processes change.
Processing limitationWe collect only what is needed to answer enquiries and carry out work, on a lawful basis, and mostly directly from you.
Purpose specificationEach purpose is set out in our Privacy Policy, and records are kept no longer than needed.
Further processing limitationWe do not use your information for purposes unrelated to why you gave it to us.
Information qualityWe confirm details with you when you book, and correct them whenever you ask.
OpennessOur Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy and this page explain what we collect and why, and our PAIA information is published.

Data subject participationYou can access, correct and delete your information as described below.

04 Your rights

  • Access - to ask whether we hold your information and to receive a record of it (section 23).
  • Correction and deletion - to ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading or unlawfully obtained (section 24).
  • Objection - to object, on reasonable grounds, to processing, and at any time to processing for direct marketing (section 11(3)).
  • Direct marketing - not to receive electronic direct marketing without your consent (section 69).
  • Complaint - to complain to the Information Regulator (section 74).

05 Making a request

  1. Email venom@venomracing.co.za with the subject "POPIA request", or deliver it to our workshop. Formal requests can use the prescribed forms in the POPIA Regulations: Form 1 to object and Form 2 to request correction or deletion.
  2. Tell us what you are asking for, and give enough detail for us to find the information (for example, the phone number or email you used).
  3. We will confirm your identity before releasing or changing any information, to protect you.
  4. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with any period set by law. Access requests are processed under the PAIA procedure.

We do not charge to correct or delete your information, or to confirm whether we hold it.

06 Operators and transfers

Service providers who process information for us (such as our database and website hosts) do so under written terms that require them to keep it confidential and secure, as sections 20 and 21 require. Where information is processed outside South Africa, we comply with section 72. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
